Who we are

A distributor built around the site schedule

Technolite TRD FZE supplies electrical and lighting materials to contractors, consultants and facilities teams across the United Arab Emirates.

The range runs from HV and LV cable through trays, trunking, glands, lugs and conduit, into switchgear, industrial plugs and the full lighting catalogue — all from manufacturers whose approvals hold up under consultant review.

Bring us a bill of quantities or a single replacement part. Either way you get a price, a stock position and a delivery date, not a promise to check.

  • 01

    Matched to spec

    Send the specification and we come back with the exact item or a documented equivalent, with datasheets attached.

  • 02

    One order, one delivery

    Cable, containment, accessories and fittings ship together instead of arriving from four separate suppliers.

  • 03

    Project pricing

    Volume rates for tendered work, with the same account contact from bid stage through to final delivery.

  • 04

    Emirates-wide delivery

    Dispatch across Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi and the northern emirates, including urgent site drops.
