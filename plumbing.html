The range

What we supply

Six groups covering a full plumbing scope. Brands and pressure ratings matched to your specification — tell us the system and we quote the equivalent.

PIP

Pipe & fittings

  • UPVC pressure pipe and fittings
  • PPR hot and cold water systems
  • CPVC pipe and fittings
  • GI and MS pipe, sockets and nipples
  • Copper tube and press fittings
  • Flexible connectors and hoses
VLV

Valves & controls

  • Gate, globe and check valves
  • Ball valves — brass and PVC
  • Butterfly and balancing valves
  • Pressure reducing valves
  • Float and angle valves
  • Strainers and non-return valves
SAN

Sanitary ware

  • WC suites and cisterns
  • Wash basins and pedestals
  • Mixers, taps and shower sets
  • Urinals and flush valves
  • Sinks and laundry troughs
  • Accessories and fixing kits
DRN

Drainage

  • UPVC soil and waste pipe
  • Floor drains and gully traps
  • Channel drains and gratings
  • Cleanouts and inspection chambers
  • Bottle and P-traps
  • Roof outlets and rainwater goods
PMP

Pumps & water systems

  • Booster and pressure pumps
  • Submersible and sump pumps
  • Circulation pumps
  • Water heaters — storage and instant
  • Pressure vessels and tanks
  • Filters and softeners
INS

Insulation & sealing

  • Pipe insulation — closed cell and rockwool
  • Insulation tape and cladding
  • Thread sealants and PTFE tape
  • Pipe clamps, hangers and brackets
  • Silicone and polyurethane sealants
  • Gaskets and jointing sheet
